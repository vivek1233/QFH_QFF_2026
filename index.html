About the Event

What is IBM Qiskit Fall Fest?

IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is IBM's flagship annual quantum computing festival — presented by IBM Quantum and Quantum for Humanity Org.

Spanning 5 full days — November 16–20, 2026, the festival takes participants on a journey from quantum computing Beginners all the way through to Advanced research-level content. Sessions are structured to progressively build knowledge, making this accessible to students, developers, researchers, and curious minds alike. Informations and learning materials are drawn from the official IBM Quantum Learning platform.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before registering.

Is IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 free?
Yes — completely free. All 5 days of sessions, workshops, and the hackathon are free to attend. Register via the Google Form above to receive your confirmation, session links, IBM Quantum credits, and participation certificate.
Do I need prior quantum computing experience?
No. Day 1 is fully beginner-friendly and starts from zero. The festival is designed to take you from Beginner (Day 1) through Intermediate (Day 2), Applied (Day 3), all the way to Advanced (Day 4). Content is drawn from the official IBM Quantum Learning platform.
How do I register?
Click the "Register Free" button anywhere on this page, or go directly to the Google Form registration. Fill in your details and you will receive a confirmation email with all session links.
How do I join the community Discord?
Join the Quantum for Humanity Discord server at discord.gg/UgF3PaEax. This is where you can find hackathon teammates, ask questions to mentors, get live event updates, and stay connected with the global quantum community year-round.
Can I attend online? Are sessions recorded?
Yes. All sessions are live-streamed globally and fully recorded. You can attend from anywhere in the world. On-demand recordings will be available after each day for registered participants.
What is the Hackathon and how do I participate?
The 48-Hour Quantum for Humanity Hackathon kicks off on Day 1 (Nov 16) and finals are on Day 5 (Nov 20). Build quantum solutions to real-world humanitarian problems across 4 domains: healthcare, climate, education, and financial inclusion. All registered participants get free IBM Quantum hardware credits. Join the Discord to find a team.
What is the connection to IBM Quantum Learning?
All educational content at Fall Fest 2026 is drawn from and aligned with the IBM Quantum Learning platform. Participants can follow along with official IBM Quantum Learning modules and earn IBM Quantum certifications through the event workshops.
How do I publish this website via GitHub Pages?
Push the QFH_QFF_2026 repository to GitHub. Go to Settings → Pages → Branch: main → Save. Ensure the .nojekyll file is included. Your site will go live within minutes at yourusername.github.io/QFH_QFF_2026/.
