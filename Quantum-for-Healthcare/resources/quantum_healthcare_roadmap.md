## Where We Are Today (2024–2025): NISQ Era

**Near-term practical healthcare applications:**

### Molecular Simulation (VQE)
- Small molecules (< 20 atoms) on NISQ hardware: H₂, LiH, BeH₂
- Drug-target binding fragment calculations
- Reaction pathway scanning for known pharmacophores

### Quantum Machine Learning
- Tumour classification from radiological features (breast, lung cancer)
- Genomic variant classification with quantum kernels
- Drug-drug interaction prediction on small datasets

### Combinatorial Optimisation (QAOA)
- ICU bed allocation for small hospitals (< 30 patients)
- Surgical schedule optimisation for a single theatre
- Ambulance routing for local emergency services
