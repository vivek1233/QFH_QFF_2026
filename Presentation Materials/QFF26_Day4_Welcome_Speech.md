# IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 - Day 4 Welcome Speech

**Day 4 - Thursday 26 November 2026 - Research & Industry Applications**
**Speaker:** Vivekanand Choudhry, Lead Organizer
**Length:** about 3 minutes when read at a relaxed pace. Times are IST.

---

## Speech

Good morning, good afternoon and good evening, everyone, wherever you are joining from.

Welcome to **Day 4** of the IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, our *Quantum for Humanity* festival.

Thank you for yesterday. Day 3 showed us why quantum-safe cryptography matters and how organisations can start the move to it today. Thank you to our four speakers, and to everyone who asked questions in the chat and on Discord.

Today is **Research and Industry Applications**. We go from the research frontier to real industry problems, and it is the most advanced day of the festival.

Here is how our day looks:

- At **09:30**, **Ravindranath Nemani** opens with *Geometric Quantum Machine Learning*, models that use the symmetry of a problem.
- At **12:00**, **Guncha Malik** from IBM looks at *Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing: The Next Decade*, and IBM's roadmap to large fault-tolerant systems.
- At **14:30**, **Pradeep Lamba** shows how quantum simulation could help with *drug discovery for TB and malaria*.
- At **15:30**, **Puneet Gandhi** speaks on *Quantum Computing in Telecom*.
- At **16:30**, **Guncha Malik** returns with *Quantum Computing in Finance*. At the same time, our **hackathon midpoint mentorship round** runs for the top teams.
- And at **19:30**, **Sean Wagner** from IBM closes the day with *Sample-based Quantum Diagonalization*, a method for quantum chemistry problems.

A few reminders before we begin.

**The hackathon code freeze is tonight.** It is **Thursday, 26 November at 23:59 IST**. Please submit your work on time. If you still need help, our mentors are in the **#hackathon-teams** channel on Discord.

**Use the same Teams link** for Days 3, 4 and 5. It is on the website and in your email, so you do not need a new one.

**Ask your questions.** Please use the chat and the Discord community throughout the day.

And a preview for tomorrow, **Day 5**. We start at **09:30** with the hackathon finals and live presentations, then the award ceremony, the Quantum for Humanity manifesto launch, the community showcase, and the closing keynote from **Adam Byrne** at **16:30**.

Thank you again for being part of this. Quantum computing will only change the world for the better if we build it together.

Let us begin Day 4. Over to **Ravindranath Nemani**.
