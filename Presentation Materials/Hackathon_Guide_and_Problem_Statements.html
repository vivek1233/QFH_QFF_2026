🎯 The 4 Grand Challenge Problem Statements

Track 1: Healthcare & Life Sciences

Targeted Molecular Simulation for Drug Discovery

Challenge: Simulate molecular ground states and binding affinities for small molecule drug candidates against infectious diseases (e.g., Tuberculosis or Malaria) using Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) or Hamiltonian simulation.

Expectations: Optimize circuit depth using Qiskit Runtime Estimator, apply error mitigation (ZNE / Twirled Readout Error Extinction), and compare runtime/accuracy against classical Hartree-Fock baselines.

Track 2: Climate, Energy & Agriculture

Grid Optimization & Catalytic Carbon Capture Modeling

Challenge: Formulate a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem using QAOA for renewable energy load balancing across power microgrids OR model electronic structures of metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) for carbon capture.

Expectations: Demonstrate parameterized ansatz optimization, analyze convergence against graph density, and provide actionable real-world deployment metrics.

Track 3: Financial Inclusion & Resilient Markets

Quantum Portfolio Optimization & Fraud Detection via QSVM

Challenge: Implement Quantum Support Vector Machines (QSVM) with parameterized quantum kernels to classify fraudulent transactions OR optimize microfinance loan portfolios under strict risk-return constraints.

Expectations: Compare quantum kernel trick performance against classical RBF kernels on high-dimensional noisy transaction datasets.

Track 4: Quantum Safe & Digital Infrastructure

Automated Cryptographic Discovery & PQC Migration

Challenge: Design an automated scanner or cryptographic inventory pipeline that detects vulnerable asymmetric cryptography (RSA/ECC) across enterprise codebases and maps replacement with NIST-approved Post-Quantum Cryptography algorithms (ML-KEM / ML-DSA).

Expectations: Provide an architectural migration blueprint and functional POC demonstrating hybrid classical/PQC key exchange.
