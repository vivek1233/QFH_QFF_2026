Our Mission

What is Quantum for Humanity?

Quantum for Humanity (QfH) is a global open initiative co-founded in partnership with IBM Quantum, dedicated to one belief: quantum computing must benefit all of humanity — not just those with access to cutting-edge laboratories.

We build bridges between quantum science and the real-world challenges faced by billions of people — in healthcare, food security, clean energy, education, and financial access.

The India Chapter, headquartered in New Delhi and active at IITs, IISc, and universities across Bharat, is the largest QfH chapter in the world — reflecting India's unique position at the intersection of quantum potential and humanitarian need.

Six Pillars

Our Focus Areas

Six pillars where quantum computing can create transformative human impact — with special focus on India's most urgent challenges.

💊
Healthcare & Drug Discovery
Quantum simulation to accelerate drug development for TB, malaria, cancer, and antibiotic resistance — diseases that disproportionately affect India and the Global South.
India Priority: TB & Malaria
🌿
Agriculture & Food Security
Quantum optimisation for crop yield prediction, nitrogen fixation modelling, irrigation scheduling, and supply chain efficiency to feed India's 1.4 billion people.
India Priority: Farmer Welfare
🌞
Climate & Clean Energy
Quantum algorithms for carbon capture simulation, solar cell optimisation, smart grid management, and climate modelling to accelerate India's net-zero transition by 2070.
India Priority: Net Zero 2070
🏭
Financial Inclusion
Quantum-powered risk modelling and fraud detection to bring banking, insurance, and credit access to India's 400 million unbanked citizens through Jan Dhan and beyond.
India Priority: Jan Dhan
🎓
Education & Access
Quantum education in Hindi and regional languages. Open-source Qiskit curricula for Indian schools, ITIs, and Tier 2/3 colleges. Free IBM Quantum learning pathways for all.
India Priority: Quantum Bharat
🔒
Cybersecurity & Privacy
Post-quantum cryptography research to protect India's digital infrastructure, Aadhaar data, UPI transactions, and critical government systems from future quantum threats.
India Priority: Digital India
India Chapter

Quantum for Humanity India

The India Chapter of Quantum for Humanity is the initiative's largest and most active chapter globally. Headquartered in New Delhi and operating across 30+ institutions, it is aligned with India's National Quantum Mission (NQM) — the INR 6000 Crore government programme to make India a global quantum leader by 2031.

We partner directly with IITs, IISc, TIFR, NITs, and the Department of Science & Technology to translate quantum research into real Indian humanitarian outcomes.

QfH India Manifesto 2026

The Quantum for Humanity Pledge

"Quantum computing is not merely a scientific achievement — it is a moral responsibility. We, the quantum community of India and the world, pledge to ensure that this technology serves all of humanity, not just the privileged few."
Principle 1
Quantum research must address real human suffering — disease, poverty, hunger, climate.
Principle 2
Access to quantum education must be free, multilingual, and available to every student in Bharat.
Principle 3
Open-source quantum tools must remain open. No paywalls on humanity's most powerful science.
Principle 4
Quantum innovation must be governed ethically, with Indian data sovereignty and citizen privacy protected.
Principle 5
The global quantum community must actively include voices from Africa, Asia, and Latin America.
Principle 6
We measure success not in papers published, but in lives improved by quantum science.
