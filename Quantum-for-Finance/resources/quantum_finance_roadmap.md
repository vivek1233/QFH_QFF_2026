# Quantum Finance Roadmap
## Quantum for Finance — Quantum for Humanity

---

## Where We Are Today (2024–2025): NISQ Era

Current IBM Quantum hardware (Eagle, Heron, Flamingo processors) operates in the **Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ)** regime:
- 100–1000+ physical qubits
- Limited coherence times; error rates ~0.1–1% per gate
- No full error correction yet
- Best suited for **hybrid classical-quantum algorithms** (QAOA, VQE, QML)

**Practical finance applications today:**
- Small-scale portfolio optimisation (< 20 assets)
- Derivative pricing with QAE (quadratic speedup demonstrated)
- Quantum kernel classification on small datasets
- Risk factor simulation

---

## Near Term (2025–2027): Early Fault Tolerance

IBM Quantum roadmap targets **10,000+ physical qubits** with early error correction:
- **Logical qubits** with surface codes
- 100–1000x reduction in effective error rates
- First demonstrations of quantum advantage in finance

**Finance applications unlocked:**
- Portfolio optimisation for 100+ assets (pension fund scale)
- Real-time options pricing with full QAE speedup
- Credit risk modelling for large loan portfolios
- High-frequency trading signal generation

---

## Medium Term (2027–2030): Fault-Tolerant Era

IBM roadmap: **100,000+ physical qubits**, fully fault-tolerant logical qubits:
- Shor's algorithm becomes practical (cryptography implications)
- Grover search at scale → database search speedup for compliance
- Full quantum Monte Carlo for complex derivatives (CVA, XVA)

**Finance applications:**
- Real-time global risk aggregation
- Quantum-accelerated credit scoring for 1B+ people
- Post-quantum cryptographic standards deployment
- Quantum-enhanced regulatory stress testing
